Privacy Policy

ID Photo Beauty（最美-证件照）· iOS & Android

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · Effective date: 25 September 2026

In one sentence: every photo you take or choose is processed entirely on your own device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your photos, your face data, or any other personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. The app has no account system, no sign-in, no cloud service and no backend server. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifier or any other personal data, because the app never sends us anything.

2. Camera, photos and face data

  • Camera. Used only while you are on the photo-taking screen, so you can take a picture.
  • Photo library (read). Used only to let you pick the photo you want to edit.
  • Photo library (add). Used only to save the finished photo back to your library.
  • Face detection. The app checks whether there is a single frontal face so it can warn you about submissions that would be rejected. This detection runs locally with an on-device model. Face data is never uploaded and is never stored beyond the current editing session.
  • All processing — face check, background removal, retouching, outfit swap, composition and export — runs on your device. Your photos never leave it.

3. What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers stays in the app's private sandbox on your device:

  • photos you exported (saved to your own photo library, under your control);
  • an optional list of recent creations (locally generated thumbnails), for the in-app history;
  • your favorites, custom sizes and export preferences.

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it.

4. Network access

The app works completely offline: airplane mode does not limit any editing or export feature. The only network communication the app performs is done by Apple's App Store or Google Play billing system, and only if you choose to leave an optional tip. In that case the store processes the payment — we never receive your name, card number or any payment details, and we only learn whether a purchase completed, so the app can thank you.

5. Analytics, advertising and tracking

No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking of any kind. The app does not use the advertising identifier, does not track you across other apps or websites, and therefore never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. We show no ads.

6. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to work: camera, photo library read, and photo library add. You can review or revoke any of them at any time in your device settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone. If you edit a photo of a child — for example for a school application — that photo is processed on your device exactly like any other photo and is never uploaded.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Data management lets you clear your history and favorites, and change or remove stored preferences.
  • Your exported photos are ordinary files in your photo library — delete them there whenever you like.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its local data from your device.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Third-party components

The app includes open-source libraries for on-device AI and image processing (for example ONNX Runtime, SkiaSharp, GPUPixel and .NET MAUI components). They run locally as part of the app and do not collect or transmit personal data. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in the app? Please email devin_contact@163.com.

隐私政策

最美-证件照 · iOS 与 Android

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 25 日 · 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日

一句话说明：你拍摄或选择的每一张照片，全部在你自己的设备上处理。 我们不会收集、上传、出售或分享你的照片、人脸数据或任何其他个人信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

没有。本应用没有账号体系、没有登录、没有云服务、没有后端服务器。我们不会收集你的姓名、 邮箱、手机号、通讯录、位置、广告标识符或任何其他个人数据，因为应用从不向我们发送任何内容。

2. 相机、照片与人脸数据

  • 相机：仅在你处于拍摄界面时使用，用于拍照。
  • 相册（读取）：仅用于让你挑选要编辑的照片。
  • 相册（写入）：仅用于把成品照片保存回你的相册。
  • 人脸检测：用于判断是否为单人正面照，以便提醒你可能被退回的照片。检测在本地由端侧模型完成， 人脸数据不会被上传，也不会在本次编辑之外被保存。
  • 所有人脸检测、AI 抠图、美颜、换装、合成与导出均在你的设备上运行，照片不会离开设备。

3. 数据存储位置

应用记住的所有内容都只保存在你设备上的应用私有沙盒中：

  • 你导出的成品照片（保存在你自己的相册，由你掌控）；
  • 可选的"最近制作"记录（本地生成的缩略图）；
  • 你的收藏、自定义尺寸与导出偏好设置。

这些内容不会传给我们或任何第三方，我们也没有任何途径访问它们。

4. 网络访问

本应用完全离线可用：飞行模式下所有编辑与导出功能不受影响。唯一的网络通讯由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 的结算系统发起，且仅在你主动选择打赏作者时发生。支付由商店完成，我们不会收到你的姓名、 卡号或任何支付信息，只会在购买完成后得知交易是否成功，以便向你致谢。

5. 统计、广告与跟踪

无统计 SDK、无广告 SDK、无崩溃上报 SDK、无任何形式的跟踪。应用不使用广告标识符，不跨应用或网站跟踪你， 因此也从不申请 App Tracking Transparency 权限。应用内没有广告。

6. 权限

应用只申请运行所必需的权限：相机、相册读取、相册写入。你可以随时在系统设置中查看或关闭。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，且不向任何人收集数据。如果你编辑的是儿童照片（例如入学报名照）， 该照片与其他照片一样仅在你的设备上处理，不会被上传。

8. 你的选择与数据删除

  • 应用内：设置 → 数据管理 可清空历史与收藏，并可修改或删除已保存的偏好设置。
  • 导出的照片是你相册中的普通文件，随时可在相册中删除。
  • 卸载应用会删除它在设备上的全部本地数据。

由于我们从不接收你的数据，因此没有需要向我们请求或由我们删除的内容。

9. 第三方组件

应用包含用于端侧 AI 与图像处理的开源库（如 ONNX Runtime、SkiaSharp、GPUPixel 及 .NET MAUI 组件）， 它们作为应用的一部分在本地运行，不收集或传输个人数据。应用内购买由 Apple 或 Google 按其各自的隐私政策处理。

10. 政策变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面及顶部日期；重要变更也会在应用的版本说明中说明。

11. 联系我们

对本政策或应用隐私有疑问，欢迎邮件联系 devin_contact@163.com。
